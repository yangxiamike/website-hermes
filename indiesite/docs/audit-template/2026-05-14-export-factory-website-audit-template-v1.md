## 0. 模板用途

本模板用于诊断中国出口工厂现有英文独立站，重点判断网站是否能帮助海外买家完成以下决策：

1. 这家公司是否真实、可信、可联系？
2. 产品是否符合我的采购规格、应用和质量要求？
3. 工厂是否具备稳定制造、质检、定制和出口交付能力？
4. 我是否知道下一步如何询价，并且询价成本足够低？

本模板不是普通 UI 设计点评，也不是泛 SEO 报告。它服务于一个核心目标：

> 把工厂真实制造能力翻译成海外买家能理解、能信任、愿意询盘的网站资产。

## 7. 英文表达诊断 English Expression Audit

### 7.1 判断标准

英文不是越华丽越好，而是要让海外买家快速理解产品、能力、限制和下一步动作。

检查点：

- □ 是否有明显机器翻译感
- □ 是否过度使用空泛形容词
- □ 是否符合 B2B 采购语言
- □ 是否准确表达规格、材料、标准和应用
- □ 是否避免中文直译
- □ 是否避免夸张承诺
- □ CTA 是否清晰自然

### 7.2 常见问题

| 问题类型 | 示例 | 风险 |
|---|---|---|
| 空泛口号 | best quality, best price | 不可信，像低端模板站 |
| 中文直译 | our company insists on quality first | 不自然，专业度下降 |
| 缺少采购信息 | high quality products | 买家无法判断是否匹配需求 |
| 过度承诺 | 100% perfect, guaranteed best | 增加信任风险 |
| AI 味明显 | buyer-focused solutions for global success | 像模板，不像真实工厂 |

### 7.3 英文改写原则

- 用具体产品替代抽象口号
- 用规格、材料、应用、标准支撑可信度
- 用真实流程替代“专业服务”
- 用采购动作引导询盘
- 不承诺无法证明的结果

示例：

```text
Weak:
We provide high quality products with competitive price and excellent service.

Stronger:
We supply CNC-machined aluminum housings for industrial equipment manufacturers, with material traceability, dimensional inspection and export packing for small-batch and repeat orders.
```

## 17. 报告语气规范

应使用：

- 专业、具体、买家视角
- 帮客户看见问题，而不是批评客户
- 用“影响海外买家判断”解释问题
- 用“建议优先改造”替代“必须重做”

避免使用：

- 你的官网很差
- 这个页面完全不行
- 你们英文太烂
- 保证询盘提升
- 保证 Google 排名
- 我们可以让你超过同行

推荐语气：

```text
The current page contains basic company information, but it does not yet give overseas buyers enough evidence to evaluate manufacturing capability, quality control, or quotation readiness. We recommend restructuring this page around factory capability, QC process and RFQ guidance.
```

## 18. 可复用结论句库

### 18.1 首页问题

```text
The homepage explains what the company sells, but it does not clearly show why an overseas buyer should trust this supplier for repeated or project-based purchasing.
```

### 18.2 产品页问题

```text
The product page is visually present, but it lacks the technical and purchasing information buyers need before sending an RFQ, such as material options, size range, standards, applications and testing requirements.
```

### 18.3 About 页面问题

```text
The About page introduces the company in general terms, but it does not provide enough factory-level evidence, such as production capability, QC process, equipment, export experience or documentation support.
```

### 18.4 英文表达问题

```text
Many sentences use generic marketing language. Rewriting them with product-specific, application-specific and process-specific details would make the website more credible to technical and procurement buyers.
```

### 18.5 询盘路径问题

```text
The website has contact information, but it does not guide buyers on what specifications or documents to send, which may reduce the number of qualified inquiries.
```
