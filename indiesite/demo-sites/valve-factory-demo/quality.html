Quotation

Send valve specifications before quotation

To quote accurately, include valve type, size, pressure class, body material, seat material, end connection, working medium, temperature, quantity, destination port, and required documents.

  • For project orders: attach drawing, standard, or datasheet if available.
  • For replacement orders: send photos of nameplate, flange, and installation space.
  • For OEM orders: confirm marking, packing, document language, and inspection requirements.
