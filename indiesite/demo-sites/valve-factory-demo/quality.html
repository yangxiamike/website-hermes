Quality control

Inspection records for pressure, material, dimensions, marking, and packing

Quality control is handled as inspection stages, not as a slogan. Test scope and documentation can be confirmed according to order standard and buyer requirement.

Inspection stageWhat is checkedTypical record
Incoming materialBody, bonnet, ball/disc, stem, seat, gasket, fastener material against order requirement.Material certificate, heat number, material photo.
MachiningCritical dimensions, flange drilling, threads, sealing surface, face-to-face dimension.Dimension record, inspection photo.
AssemblySeat and seal installation, stem operation, gasket matching, fastener tightening, operation check.Assembly checklist.
Pressure testShell test, seat leakage test, pressure holding time, visible leakage check.Hydrostatic or pneumatic test report.
Final inspectionMarking, nameplate, paint, surface condition, end protection, packing condition.Final inspection report and packing photos.

Quotation

Send valve specifications before quotation

To quote accurately, include valve type, size, pressure class, body material, seat material, end connection, working medium, temperature, quantity, destination port, and required documents.

  • For project orders: attach drawing, standard, or datasheet if available.
  • For replacement orders: send photos of nameplate, flange, and installation space.
  • For OEM orders: confirm marking, packing, document language, and inspection requirements.
