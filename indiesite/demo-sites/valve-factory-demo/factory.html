Factory capability

Machining, assembly, pressure testing, and export packing in one workflow

A valve order is controlled from specification review to packed shipment. The production route changes according to valve type, material, pressure class, standard, and inspection requirement.

Specification review

Confirm valve type, size, pressure class, material, medium, temperature, connection, applicable standard, and document package.

Machining control

Check critical dimensions, sealing surfaces, flange drilling, threads, face-to-face dimension, and surface finish.

Assembly and operation

Match seats, seals, stems, balls, discs, gaskets, and fasteners according to order specification. Check operation before testing.

Packing and shipment

Apply rust prevention, protect sealing surfaces, add plastic caps, mark cases, and prepare export wooden case or pallet packing.
