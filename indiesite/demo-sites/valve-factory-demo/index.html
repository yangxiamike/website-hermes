Applications

Applications commonly supplied

Valve configuration depends on medium, pressure, temperature, corrosion risk, installation position, and required documents. The same valve type may need different body material, seat material, end connection, and test standard.

Oil & gas

Pipeline isolation, tank farms, compressor stations, and fuel handling systems. Pressure class, fire-safe design, material traceability, and test reports can be confirmed by project requirement.

Chemical processing

Valve configurations selected according to medium, temperature, corrosion risk, sealing material, and maintenance requirements.

Water treatment

Valves for pumps, filtration systems, municipal pipelines, and industrial water projects. Export packing and batch delivery are available.

Quotation

Send valve specifications before quotation

To quote accurately, include valve type, size, pressure class, body material, seat material, end connection, working medium, temperature, quantity, destination port, and required documents.

  • For project orders: attach drawing, standard, or datasheet if available.
  • For replacement orders: send photos of nameplate, flange, and installation space.
  • For OEM orders: confirm marking, packing, document language, and inspection requirements.
