Before / After case asset

From a thin old English website to a procurement-ready valve factory site

This internal sample shows the difference between a typical outdated export-factory English website and a rebuilt site that helps overseas buyers check products, manufacturing capability, quality control, documents, packing, and quotation inputs.

Disclosure: this is a fictional sample case based on common old-site problems. It does not use real customer data, real logos, or unauthorized factory materials.

Old site diagnosis

What the old site failed to answer

Many factory websites list product names but do not help a buyer make a purchasing judgment. The result is a site that looks “present” but cannot reduce sourcing risk.

Before

Product pages were only category lists

Ball valve, gate valve, check valve, and butterfly valve were listed without size range, pressure class, material options, seat materials, connection standards, or application notes.

Before

No manufacturing proof

The old site said “professional manufacturer” but showed no production workflow, machining control points, assembly process, pressure testing, or export packing details.

Before

No clear RFQ path

The contact page asked for a message only. It did not guide buyers to send valve type, size, pressure, material, medium, temperature, quantity, destination port, or document requirements.

Upgrade map

Five concrete changes made in the renovation

The upgrade is not cosmetic decoration. It reorganizes the website around how overseas buyers evaluate a valve supplier: product fit, factory reliability, quality control, document readiness, and quotation clarity.

Trust expression

The upgraded site speaks like a real export factory

Instead of polished but empty marketing language, the rebuilt page uses the words procurement teams need when checking a valve supplier. It does not promise rankings, leads, or guaranteed orders; it makes the factory easier to evaluate.

Specific product language

Valve type, pressure class, body material, seat material, end connection, medium, temperature, and standard.

Visible quality checkpoints

Pressure testing, dimensional inspection, operation check, marking, document preparation, and export packing inspection.

Clear quotation inputs

A buyer knows what to send before asking for price, so the inquiry becomes easier for the factory to process.

Case takeaway

Renovation goal: make real manufacturing capability easier to trust

This sample asset can be used on the service website to explain the old-site renovation offer: we do not just redesign the surface; we rebuild the website around buyer questions, factory proof, and usable inquiry information.
