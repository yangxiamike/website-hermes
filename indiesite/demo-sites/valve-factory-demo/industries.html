Applications

Valve applications by working condition

Industry pages help buyers verify that a supplier understands the operating environment. Valve choice changes with medium, pressure, temperature, corrosion risk, installation position, and document requirement.

O&G

Oil & gas

Pipeline isolation, tank farms, compressor stations, metering skids, and fuel handling systems.

  • Typical products: ball valves, gate valves, check valves, strainers.
  • Key concerns: pressure class, fire-safe design, anti-static device, material traceability.
  • Documents: MTC, pressure test report, inspection photos, packing list.
CHEM

Chemical processing

Process lines where medium compatibility, corrosion risk and temperature determine material and seat selection.

  • Typical products: ball valves, globe valves, check valves, actuated valves.
  • Key concerns: body material, seat / seal compatibility, leakage class, maintenance access.
  • Documents: material list, datasheet, test report, marking photos.
WATER

Water & wastewater

Pump stations, filtration systems, municipal pipelines, desalination and industrial water projects.

  • Typical products: butterfly valves, check valves, gate valves, strainers.
  • Key concerns: coating, rubber liner, flange standard, actuator, batch delivery.
  • Documents: packing photos, coating confirmation, pressure test report.
POWER

Power generation

Steam, cooling water, fuel, auxiliary systems and plant maintenance orders.

  • Typical products: globe valves, gate valves, check valves, ball valves.
  • Key concerns: temperature, pressure, seat material, operation mode, spare parts.
  • Documents: datasheet, dimension check, MTC, test report.
MARINE

Marine & offshore

Shipbuilding, offshore utilities, seawater systems and maintenance replacement orders.

  • Typical products: butterfly valves, ball valves, check valves, strainers.
  • Key concerns: seawater corrosion, compact installation, class requirement, packing.
  • Documents: photos of marking, coating, packing and dimensions.
OEM

OEM equipment builders

Batch valves supplied with private label, repeat packaging, drawings and agreed inspection routine.

  • Typical products: customized ball valves, actuated valves, small-bore valves.
  • Key concerns: repeatability, marking, packaging, drawings, sample approval.
  • Documents: sample report, dimension sheet, packing standard.
How to send an application RFQ

Application details reduce quotation errors

For each inquiry, buyers should include medium, pressure, temperature, installation position, material preference, connection standard, quantity, destination port, and required documents.

Technical RFQ

Send working conditions before quotation

To quote accurately, include valve type, size, pressure class, body and trim material, seat material, end connection, medium, temperature, quantity, destination port, and required documents.

  • For project orders: attach drawings, datasheets, or project standards if available.
  • For replacement orders: send photos of nameplate, flange, actuator, and installation space.
  • For OEM orders: confirm marking, private label, packing, inspection and document language.
